About Us

Our Story

Wacdonalds was founded in 2077 in Night City by a swamp-loving visionary who believed fast food should be affordable, flavourful, and unforgettable. Starting as a single late-night burger stand, Wacdonalds quickly became a popular stop for people looking for comfort food in a busy futuristic city. Over the years, Wacdonalds has grown into a fun and recognizable food brand known for its burgers, fries, drinks, and playful personality. While our menu has changed, our goal has stayed the same: to serve fast, satisfying meals that bring people together.
