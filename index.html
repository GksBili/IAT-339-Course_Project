Basic Branding Ideas

Basic branding concepts for the WacDonald's brand.

What does the company do?

WacDonald's is a fast-food restaurant brand that offers burgers, fries, drinks, and combo meals inspired by classic North American fast-food culture. The company focuses on fast service, bold flavor, affordable meals, and a playful reversed-brand identity.

Who is the company to potential customers?

Potential customers view WacDonald's as a convenient and affordable option for quick meals.

Why might this company entice someone to purchase a product or service

WacDonald's entices customers with its combination of convenience, affordability, and consistent quality. The company's fast service and interesting food also contribute to its appeal.
